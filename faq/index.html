04

Pricing & payment

Pricing depends on the selected design, quantity, and paper options. You'll be able to review your order details and pricing before confirming your order.

Matching envelopes are included with printed invitations. Optional envelope addressing is available for an additional fee. Local pickup is free, and delivery within the eruv is $10.

We'll confirm the total and payment arrangements when your order is finalized. Payment is due when your completed order is picked up or delivered.

05

Timing & production

Most orders are ready within 5–7 business days after final design approval. Larger orders or special paper requests may require additional time. We'll provide an estimated completion date when your order is confirmed.

Have an event coming up soon? Let us know your date. We'll do our best to accommodate rush requests, depending on our current production schedule.

We recommend ordering at least 3–4 weeks before your event. This leaves time for personalization, review, printing, and getting your invitations to your guests.

06

Changes & quality

If you need to make a change, contact us as soon as possible. Changes can generally be accommodated before printing begins. Once production has started, changes may not be possible.

Yes. Before approving your final design, carefully review all names, dates, times, addresses, Hebrew and English spelling, and other details. Printing begins only after you approve the final version.

We stand behind the quality of our work. If there's a printing defect or an error on our part, contact us within 7 days of receiving your order so we can review the issue and make it right.

Orders may be canceled before printing begins. Once an order has entered production, cancellations and refunds may no longer be possible. Please contact us as soon as possible if you need to cancel.
